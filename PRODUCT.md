# Product (seed for the impeccable skill; sources: docs/01_PRD.md, 03_UI_UX_DESIGN.md, 12_FRONTEND_SPEC.md)

**Product:** FinSight, an IPO X-Ray for Indian retail investors. It reads an IPO offer document (RHP and final prospectus), shows a cited fact sheet, and answers questions in English and Hindi with every number checked against the pages (matches / couldn't check / doesn't match).

**Users:** Priya, a first-time IPO applicant (26, salaried, comfortable in Hindi, never opened an RHP); Rahul, a commerce student learning to read offer documents; an examiner who wants to see the models and the numbers.

**Voice:** a calm, well-informed friend who has actually read the prospectus. Plain words, short sentences, sentence case, no hype, no exclamation marks, no emoji. Never advice, never ratings, never predictions.

**Modes per surface:** Landing = Persuade. Library, Workspace, Model Lab = Operate. How it works, About = Read.

**Hard constraints:** local open-weight models only; verdict colours reserved for verdicts; the document page is the hero; copy comes from docs/12_FRONTEND_SPEC.md verbatim.

**Anti-references:** generic AI landing pages, crypto-style neon, bank-app blue gradients, identical card grids, all-caps eyebrow labels.
