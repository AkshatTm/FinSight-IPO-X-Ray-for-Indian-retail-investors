# Code of conduct

FinSight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), in short:

- Be respectful and welcoming; disagree about code, not about people.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else connected with the project.
- No publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

Problems can be reported privately to the maintainer through the repository's Security tab (**Report a vulnerability**) or an issue asking for a private contact. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments or contributions that break these rules and may block repeat offenders.
