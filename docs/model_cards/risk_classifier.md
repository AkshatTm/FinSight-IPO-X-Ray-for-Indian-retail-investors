# Risk classifier (DeBERTa-v3-base; large not adopted)

!!! info "Status in v1.1"
    **Shipped:** seed 42 of the base model, exported to ONNX fp32 and run on the CPU in the upload
    stage `risks_scored` (`models/risk_classifier/onnx/`). It gives each risk a category and a
    seriousness band. Not adopted: DeBERTa-v3-large and the int8 export (below). The other seeds are
    kept in the private `AkshatTm/finsight-weights-archive` repo (see [Operations](../operations/index.md#model-weights-and-the-archive)).

- **Base model:** `microsoft/deberta-v3-base` (MIT), fine-tuned for 10-way sequence classification; served as ONNX (fp32) on the CPU
- **Owner:** Akshat · **Notebook:** `notebooks/b2_classifier_base_kaggle.ipynb` (generated by `scripts/make_classifier_notebooks.py`, run on Kaggle with `scripts/classifier_kaggle.py`)
- **Version:** the runs recorded in `eval_results/b/classifier_base.json`

## Intended use

Puts each risk factor of an offer document into one of 10 categories (financial, debt, customers and suppliers, competition, legal, and so on) so the report can group risks and the risk level can count them by kind. It reads the text of one risk at a time.

**Out of scope:** judging how serious a risk is, judging a company, and any advice. The category is a label for reading, not a verdict.

## Training data

Teacher labels (AI-made, `label_source`) on `train`-slice risks only, split 90/10 **by company** so no company is on both sides (`python -m finsight.risks.classify split`). The time split holds: the manifests `classifier_train` and `classifier_dev` list the IPOs and `tests/test_split_leakage.py` checks them. See the [teacher datasheet](../phase2/datasheets/teacher_outputs.md).

## Training procedure and evaluation

Class-weighted cross-entropy, 4 epochs, learning rate 2e-5, max length 384, three seeds (13, 42, 2026) on a Kaggle T4. The best epoch is kept by dev macro-F1; the best seed is chosen on dev. Gold-150 is for the final report only and never chooses anything. The model must beat the TF-IDF baseline on dev before it is used.

## Limitations

- The labels come from a teacher model, not from people; the classifier can only be as good as they are. Gold-150 (verified by Akshat) measures the gap.
- One annotator for the gold set; three seeds, not more.
- Trained on Indian offer-document English; risks written in other styles may be mislabelled.
- The ONNX export is checked against PyTorch on the dev rows (`onnx_check.json`): agreement 1.0, 0.56 s per risk on the laptop CPU. Dynamic int8 quantisation was tried and rejected: 5 % agreement with the default settings, 82 % at best (`eval_results/c/classifier_onnx_int8_attempt.json`).

## Ethical considerations

A category is a reading aid. A wrong category moves a risk to another group on the Risks tab and can
change which risks count as "highly serious" for the risk level, so the level always lists its
reasons and every risk keeps the document's own wording one click away. The model sees only public
offer-document text. Labels are AI-made (`label_source`), and that is disclosed wherever they are used.

## How to use and reproduce

`finsight.risks.OnnxClassifier(models/risk_classifier/onnx)` loads `model.onnx`, `tokenizer.json` and
`labels.json`. To reproduce: split (`uv run python -m finsight.risks.classify split`), run the Kaggle
notebook through `scripts/classifier_kaggle.py`, then export with `scripts/export_onnx_classifier.py`.
The steps are on [Training lanes](../operations/training-lanes.md#kaggle).
