# Risk simplifier (student)

- **Base model:** `Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507` (Apache-2.0), LoRA r=16 adapter, served as GGUF Q4_K_M (Ollama tag `finsight-student:q4`)
- **Owner:** Akshat · **Notebook:** `notebooks/colab/c2_student.ipynb` (generated by `scripts/make_colab_notebooks.py`, steps in `docs/phase3/COLAB_STEPS_student.md`), run on a Colab G4
- **Weights:** `models/student/` on the laptop (gitignored) and a private Hugging Face job repo; adapter and GGUF only, never merged fp16
- **Version:** the run recorded in `eval_results/c/student_train_metrics.json` (4B; the 8B variant is separate and not reported here)

## Intended use

Rewrites one risk factor of an Indian IPO offer document in plain English for a first-time investor: at most 60 words, short sentences, every number kept as written, the certainty of the original kept ("may" stays "may"). The product runs the post-checks (numbers, forbidden phrases, length, certainty) on every rewrite and shows the original text when a check fails.

**Out of scope:** judging whether a risk is serious, judging a company, any buy / sell / apply / avoid wording, other document types, other languages.

## Training data

About 10,300 (risk, rewrite) pairs written by the teacher (Gemma-4-31B-it, chosen by the blind bake-off, C-ADR-05) on `train`-slice risks and kept after the product post-checks and the C2.3b clean-up; 527 dev pairs, split by company. The label source is the teacher (`label_source`), not people. Test-slice and bench documents never enter training (C-ADR-02, checked by `tests/test_split_leakage.py`). Teacher datasheet: [teacher outputs](../phase2/datasheets/teacher_outputs.md).

## Training procedure

LoRA r=16 on bf16 weights, 2 epochs, 1,290 steps, effective batch 16, cosine schedule, one seed, checkpoints every 200 steps with resume. The final adapter is checkpoint-1290. The GGUF is built from the merged fp16 weights with llama.cpp and the merged folder is deleted.

## Limitations

- Labels come from a teacher model, not from people; the student copies its style and its mistakes.
- gold-50 is 50 risks from 10 test IPOs, one rater, one seed. The post-check pass rate is an automatic check of numbers, phrases, length and certainty, not of every shade of meaning.
- Readability uses a syllable heuristic; it is a comparison of means, not a per-sentence score.
- The CPU timing was taken on one 8-thread Windows laptop with other applications open; another machine will differ.
- Student runs through Ollama with the base model's chat template; `llama-cpp-python` was not used for the numbers above.
