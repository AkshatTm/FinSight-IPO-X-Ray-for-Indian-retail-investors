## Caveats (not hidden)
1. **The 2B model is weak.** In the live trick question it wrote the amount in words ("two thousand six hundred twenty-six lakh rupees") and the verifier, which reads digits, did not flag the words. Number words are not checked (a known limit of the digit verifier).
2. **The voice transcript is imperfect.** Whisper `small` heard "प्रमोटर" as "प्रमोड़त". The user sees and edits the transcript before asking (spec 7.5.6). `full` uses large-v3-turbo (CER 0.06 on E12, 9.8 s, over the 6 s target).
3. **Not run live:** the `full` profile (dense retrieval and reranker, 4 GB GPU) through the API, and a complete E7 run over the 50 dev questions with the hand-checked sample. The E7 script is in place (`finsight.evaluate.answers`); the sample needs your review.
